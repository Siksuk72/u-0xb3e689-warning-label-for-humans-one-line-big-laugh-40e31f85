One line, big laugh: a safety warning label for every human being.

> WARNING: Contents under pressure. Will insist I am fine while visibly not fine. Do not shake.

Why it wins against the brief: it reads exactly like a real printed label — "WARNING: Contents under pressure." in the clipped, imperative rhythm of a safety sticker — and the joke is pure recognition: every human has insisted "I'm fine" while visibly falling apart. The laugh lands in one read, with the turn arriving in the last few words ("Do not shake."), which treats the human exactly like the pressurised can the label describes. One idea, 93 characters, no filler, no second joke.

python3 main.py
